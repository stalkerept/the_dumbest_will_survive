3) $S_{p} = \sup_{\xi}{\sigma(f, p, i)} \quad s(p)=\inf_{\xi}{\sigma(f,p,\xi)}$
#### Доказательство:
1. $\forall{\sigma(f,p,\xi)} = \sum_{\xi=1}^{n_{p}}{f(\xi_{j})\Delta x_{j}} \leq \sum_{1}^{n_{p}}{\sup_{\Delta j}{f(x)}\Delta x_{j}}=S_{p}$
$$
\begin{align*}
\forall{\epsilon>0} \forall{j =1, \dots, n_{p}} ~ \exists{\xi_{j}':} f(\xi'_{j}) > \sup_{\Delta j}{f(x)} -\epsilon \\
\sigma(f, p, \xi') = \sum_{j = 1}^{n_{p}}{f(\xi'_{j})\Delta x_{j}} > \sum_{j=1}^{n_{p}}{(\sup_{\Delta j}{f(x)} -\epsilon)\Delta x_{j}} =  \\
= \sum_{1}^{n_{p}}{\sup_{\Delta j}{f(x)}\Delta x_{j}} - \epsilon \sum_{j=1}^{n_{p}}{\Delta x_{j}} = S_{p} - \epsilon(b-a)
\end{align*}
$$
### Продолжение
Колебание $f(x)$ на $[a; b]$
$$
\omega_{[a; b]}(f) = \omega(f, [a; b]) = \sup_{x', x'' \in [a; b]}|f(x') - f(x'')| = \sup_{x', x'' \in [a; b]}(f(x') - f(x''))
$$
**Утверждение**:
$$
\omega(f, [a; b]) = \sup_{[a; b]}f(x) - \inf_{[a; b]}f(x)
$$
**Доказательство**:
a. $$
\begin{align*}
\forall{x', x'' \in [a; b]} f(x') - f(x'') \leq \sup_{[a; b]}f(x) - \inf_{[a; b]}f(x) \implies \\
\implies  \sup(f(x') - f(x'')) = \omega(f, [a; b]) \leq M_{[a; b]} - m_{[a; b]}
\end{align*}
$$
b. (точность) 
$$
\begin{align*}
\forall{\epsilon>0} \\
 \exists{x'_{\epsilon}\in[a; b]}: f(x_{\epsilon})' > M - \frac{\epsilon}{2} \\
\exists x''_{\epsilon} \in [a; b]: f(x''_{\epsilon})<m+\frac{\epsilon}{2} \\
f(x'_{\epsilon}) - f(x''_{\epsilon}) > \left( M - \frac{\epsilon}{2} \right) - \left( m + \frac{\epsilon}{2} \right) = M - m = \epsilon
\end{align*}
$$

Свойство 4:
$$
S_{p} - s_{p} = \sum_{j=1}^{n_{p}}{(M_{j} - m_{j})\Delta x_{j}} = \sum_{j =1}^{n_{p}}{\omega(f, \Delta j)\Delta x_{j}}
$$

### Определение интегралов д'Арбу:
$$
I_{*} = \sup_{p}{s_{p}} \quad I^{*} = \inf_{p}{S_{p}}
$$Первый - нижний,  второй - верхний интеграл д'Арбу.
## Критерий интегрируемости д'Арбу
### Теорема 1:
$$
f(x) \in \mathbb{B}[a; b], f(x) \in \mathbb{R}[a; b] \Longleftrightarrow \lim\limits_{ \lambda_{p} \to 0 }{(S_{p} - s_{p})} = 0 
$$
**Доказательство**:
$$
\begin{align*}
\implies f(x) \in \mathbb{R}[a; b], \text{ т.е. } \exists{I}: \forall{\epsilon>0} \exists{\delta_{\epsilon}>0}: \forall{p}, \lambda_{p}<\delta_{\epsilon} \forall{\xi} ~ |\sigma(f, p, \xi) - I| < \epsilon \\
I - \epsilon < \sigma(f, p, \xi)< I + \epsilon \\
I - \epsilon \leq \inf_{\xi}{\sigma(f, p, \xi)} = s_{p} \leq  S_{p} = \sup_{\xi}{\sigma(f, p, \xi)} \leq I-\epsilon \\
I - \epsilon \leq s_{p} \leq S_{p} \leq I + \epsilon \\
S_{p} - s_{p} \leq 2\epsilon \implies  \lim\limits_{ \lambda_{p} \to 0 }(S_{p} - s_{p}) = 0 \\
\impliedby \lim\limits_{ \lambda_{p} \to 0 }(S_{p} - s_{p}) = 0 \implies S_{p} - s_{p} \geq I^{*} - I_{*} \geq 0 \\
\text{Т.к. }\lambda_{p}\to 0 \implies  S_{p} - s_{p} \to 0 \implies I^{*} = I_{*} = I \quad 
\end{align*}
$$
$$
\begin{align*}
\forall{\epsilon>0} ~ \forall{\xi}~ \exists{\delta_{\epsilon}>0}:\forall{p} ~ \lambda_{p}<\delta_{\epsilon} \quad 0 \leq  S_{p} - s_{p} < \epsilon \\
\text{По свойству 3:} s_{p} \leq \sigma(f, p, \xi) \leq S_{p} \implies |\sigma(f, p, \xi) - I| \leq S_{p} - s_{p} < \epsilon \implies  \\
\implies  \lim\limits_{ \lambda_{p} \to 0 }{\sigma(f, p, \xi)} = I = \int_{a}^{b}{f(x)dx} \implies f(x) \in \mathbb{R}[a; b] \quad \square
\end{align*}
$$
### Следствие:
$$
\lambda_{p} \to 0 \implies  S_{p} - s_{p} = \sum_{j = 1}^{n_{p}}{\omega(f, \Delta_{j})\Delta x_{j}} \to 0
$$

$$
f(x) \in \mathbb{B}[a; b], f(x) \in \mathbb{R}[a; b] \Longleftrightarrow I_{*} = I^{*}
$$

## Некоторые классы интегрируемых функций
### Теорема 1:
$f(x) \in \mathbb{C}[a; b] \implies f(x) \in \mathbb{R}[a; b]$
#### Доказательство (Теорема Кантора о равномерной непрерывности):
$$
\begin{align*}
f(x) \in \mathbb{C}[a; b] \implies  \forall{\epsilon >0}~\exists{\delta_{\epsilon}>0}: \forall{x', x'' \in [a; b]} ~ |f(x') - f(x'')| < \epsilon \\
\forall{\Delta \subset [a; b]}, \text{длина }\Delta < \delta_{\epsilon} \implies \sup_{x', x'' \in \Delta}{|f(x') - f(x'')|} \leq \epsilon, \text{ т.е. } \omega(f, \Delta) \leq \epsilon \\
\forall{p},  \lambda_{p} < \delta_{\epsilon} ~ 0\leq \sum_{j = 1}^{n_{p}}{\omega(f, \Delta_{j})\Delta x_{j}} \leq \epsilon \sum_{j = 1}^{n_{p}}{\Delta x_{j}} = \epsilon(b-a)
\end{align*}
$$

### Теорема 2:
$$
f(x) \in \mathbb{B}[a; b] \text{ и имеет 1 точку разрыва} \implies f(x) \in \mathbb{R}[a; b] 
$$
**Доказательство**:
$$
\begin{align*}
\exists M: \forall{x \in [a; b]} ~ |f(x)| < M \\
\forall{\epsilon>0} ~ \forall{\Delta \subset [a; b]} ~ \omega(f, \Delta) \leq  f(x') - f(x'') \leq M - (-M) = 2M \\
[a; b] \text{ без } (c - \delta; c+\delta) = [a; c-\delta] \cup [c+\delta; b] \\
\exists{\delta_{\epsilon}'}: \forall{x', x '' \in [a; c-\delta]} ~ |f(x') - f(x'')|<\epsilon \\
\exists{\delta_{\epsilon}''}: \forall{x', x '' \in [c+\delta; b]} ~ |f(x') - f(x'')|<\epsilon \\
\delta_{\epsilon} = \min{\{ \delta, \delta_{\epsilon}', \delta_{\epsilon}'' \}} \\
\forall{p}: \lambda_{p}<\delta_{\epsilon} \sum_{j=1}^{n_{p}}{\omega(f, \Delta_{j})\Delta x_{j}} = \sum_{\Delta_{j} \cap (c - \delta; c+\delta) = \emptyset}' + \sum_{\Delta_{j} \cap (c - \delta; c+\delta) \neq \emptyset}'' \\
\sum' = \sum_{\Delta_{j} \cap (c - \delta; c+\delta) = \emptyset}{\omega(f, \Delta_{j})\Delta x_{j}} < \epsilon \sum_{j=1}^{n_{p}}{\Delta x_{j}} < \epsilon(b - a) \\
\sum'' = \sum_{\Delta_{j} \cap (c - \delta; c+\delta) \neq \emptyset}{\omega(f, \Delta_{j})\Delta x_{j}} \leq 2M \sum_{\Delta_{j} \cap (c - \delta; c+\delta) \neq \emptyset} {\Delta x_{j}} < 2M * 4\delta = 8\delta M \end{align*}
$$
